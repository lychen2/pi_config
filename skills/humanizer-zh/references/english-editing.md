# English prose editing

Read [the shared editing principles](editing-principles.md) first. They own meaning,
structure, voice, protected content and delivery. This reference adds English-specific
patterns; it is not a detector of authorship.

## Local wording changes

- Prefer a simple copula when it says the same thing: “is” or “has” can replace
  ceremonial “serves as” or “boasts.” Keep a functional distinction when it matters.
- Replace padded phrases when their meaning is unchanged: “in order to” → “to,”
  “due to the fact that” → “because,” “has the ability to” → “can.”
- Inspect trailing participles such as “highlighting,” “symbolizing” or “ensuring.”
  Retain a supported causal relationship; remove an unsupported claim of significance.
- Preserve the actor when known. Use active voice if it improves clarity; passive
  voice remains appropriate when the process or result is the subject. Do not invent
  an actor to eliminate the passive.
- Use a range only when its endpoints share a meaningful scale. Otherwise name the
  topics without suggesting an ordering that the material does not establish.
- Follow the publication's rules for headings, quotation marks, hyphenation and
  abbreviations. Keep title case in proper names, real-time compound modifiers,
  en dashes in ranges, and quoted punctuation when required.
- Remove staged hooks (“Honestly?”, “Here's the thing”) only when they add ceremony
  rather than the author's intended voice. A word in an ordinary sentence is not a fault.

## Examples with unchanged facts

**Original**
> The update adds batch export, marking a revolutionary leap in productivity.

**Edited**
> The update adds batch export.

**Original**
> The controller serves as the component that has the ability to save the selected profile.

**Edited**
> The controller can save the selected profile.

**Original**
> The protagonist leaves home. The main character reaches the station. The central
> figure waits for the train.

**Edited**
> The protagonist leaves home, reaches the station and waits for the train.

Use the last compression only when sentence-level editing fits the requested style;
retain separate sentences if their pacing or emphasis matters.

## Genre and typography

A technical paragraph can remain neutral. Keep genuine first-person experience,
ambivalence and humor when present; never supply a date, location, feeling or anecdote
that the source lacks. Dashes, curly quotes, lists, parallel clauses and varied sentence
lengths are ordinary writing choices, not independent reasons to rewrite.

For long English manuscripts, identify and explain local problems against the author's
style guide. The bundled Chinese pattern counter is not calibrated for English; do not
apply its Chinese densities or reference values to English prose.
